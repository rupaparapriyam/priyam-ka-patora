# Project instructions — Other AI Ideas

This project holds Priyam's AI ideas that have been researched and come back **negative**, kept
in one place so they can be revisited deliberately rather than re-litigated by accident.

## Source of truth

- `Vault/Startup Ideas/01 - AI Chatbot Assistant/AI Chatbot Assistant.md`
- `Vault/Startup Ideas/05 - Vertical Ops Assistant (Other Industries)/Vertical Ops Assistant (Other Industries).md`

Follow `Vault/AI Collaboration Rules.md` when writing into either.

## Where they stand

**AI Chatbot Assistant** — ⚠️ Simplify & pivot; effectively dead as a generic play. 2026 industry
consensus is that horizontal AI chatbots are being absorbed by ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini directly and
only vertical, workflow-specific products survive. That conclusion is what produced the pivot to
Pathology Lab. An evolved version (agent builder + employee monitoring + insider-leak/DLP + a
private company-hosted AI, packaged as an "OS") was researched on 2026-08-29 and came back
🔴 high risk: it bundles three markets, two of which (enterprise DLP requiring SOC2/ISO27001, and
capital-intensive private-AI infra at $150k–500k in GPUs) a solo founder cannot enter. Only the
agent-builder UI layer is realistically buildable, and that space is already crowded and funded
(n8n, Gumloop, Dify, Chatbase).

**Vertical Ops Assistant (other industries)** — 🔴 Crowded across all three researched candidates
(CA/accounting, logistics, SME finance-ops), and it got worse: Suvit was acquired by Vyapar in
2026 into a funded incumbent. One untouched speculative thread remains: diagnostic and imaging
centres (radiology, dialysis chains) sit next to pathology labs and might repeat the
family-access playbook — but this has had **zero** research and is a guess, not a lead.

## How to behave in this project

These verdicts are recent and evidence-backed. The useful posture is: don't help re-open them
without new evidence. If Priyam brings a new angle, ask what has *changed* since the research —
a new competitor exit, a new regulation, a specific customer asking — before running another pass.
An idea that keeps coming back with the same shape is costing hours that `Roadmap.md` says belong
to the Pathology Lab and Ecommerce Hub.

Direct and honest over optimistic; fact-check rather than agree. Saying "this is still dead, and
here's what would have to change" is a complete and useful answer here.

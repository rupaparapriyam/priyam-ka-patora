# Ecommerce Hub — Pre-Seed Pitch Deck
Priyam · Founder · August 2026 (18 slides)

## 1. Title
**One dashboard for India's D2C brands** — AI-native analytics built for India's payment gateways, WhatsApp, and logistics carriers from day one, not bolted on later.

## 2. The Problem
Running a D2C brand means living in six different tabs:
- Google Analytics / GA4 — traffic and on-site behavior
- Meta & Google Ads — spend, CAC, creative performance
- WhatsApp (AiSensy, Wati, Interakt) — cart recovery, order updates
- Payments (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU) — settlement and RTO data
- Logistics (Shiprocket, Delhivery, XpressBees) — delivery and returns
- Spreadsheets — where it all gets stitched together, manually

Priyam lived this firsthand running SURGE, a D2C hair-styling brand.

## 3. The Solution
One dashboard, every channel, plain-language answers. Pulls GA4, ad platforms, WhatsApp, India's payment gateways, and logistics carriers into one view, then explains what changed and why.
- Cross-channel storytelling: "CAC rose 18% this week because Meta creative fatigue hit your top SKU" — not just a chart.
- India-native from day one: native data from Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, Shiprocket, Delhivery, XpressBees — the piece every global tool is missing.
- WhatsApp built in: cart recovery and order-update data live alongside ad spend and revenue.

Honest caveat: AI "plain-language insight" already exists at Polar Analytics and Lifesight — not a novel idea alone. The real verified gap is that zero major global tool supports Razorpay, Cashfree, or Indian logistics carriers natively (confirmed in Triple Whale's own integration docs). The combination — cross-channel AI storytelling + India-native data together — is the actual wedge.

## 4. Product / How It Works
Four steps: **Connect** (GA4, Meta/Google Ads, WhatsApp, India's leading payment gateways and logistics carriers) → **Understand** (engine cross-references spend, funnel, payments, delivery data) → **Explain** (plain-language findings: what changed, why, which channel) → **Act** (specific, ranked recommendations).

Example output: "RTO on your Delhi-NCR COD orders jumped to 22% this week — driven almost entirely by Shiprocket's Bluedart shipments, not Delhivery. Recommend pausing COD for Bluedart in this pincode cluster."

## 5. Integrations — Not Just Razorpay
Full connector map by category (MVP ships GA4 + one payment gateway + one logistics carrier first; this is the expansion map, not day-one scope):
- **Payments**: Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe PG
- **Logistics & shipping**: Shiprocket, Delhivery, XpressBees, Ecom Express
- **WhatsApp / BSPs**: AiSensy, Wati, Interakt, Gupshup
- **Storefront & OMS**: Shopify, WooCommerce, Unicommerce
- **Marketplaces**: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Meesho
- **Ads & analytics**: Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4

## 6. Why Consolidate
"Ten subscriptions become one dashboard." A typical D2C brand pays separately for: web/app analytics, WhatsApp automation, email/SMS automation, payment gateway dashboard, logistics dashboard, warehouse inventory & monitoring, marketplace panels (Amazon/Flipkart/Myntra), and ads reporting — 8+ tools to run one storefront. Ecommerce Hub reconciles all of it in one login, one bill, one dashboard.

**Important distinction**: not becoming a payments or logistics company — we connect to a brand's existing payment gateway, logistics provider, and marketplaces as-is (stays third-party/"out-house"). WhatsApp automation, email/SMS automation, and warehouse monitoring are built in-house, natively inside the dashboard, not resold third-party widgets.

## 7. What's Next — A Private, Brand-Trained AI
Beyond dashboards: an AI trained on the brand's own business, not the whole internet.
- Learns the business, not just the category — trained continuously on the brand's own sales, margin, inventory, customer data; gets sharper the longer a brand stays, a moat competitors can't shortcut.
- Recommends the next move, not just the last one — which SKUs to discount, which channel is under-funded, which cohort is about to churn.
- A growth co-pilot, not a reporting tool — from "here's what happened" to "here's what to do about it, and why."

Flagged plainly as a build target on the roadmap (India Scale phase), not a live feature today.

## 11. Why Now
Three shifts converging in India right now:
- D2C compounding at 24%+/yr — India's D2C market projected to nearly triple by 2031 (Mordor Intelligence).
- API access just got easier — Meta lowered its higher-access threshold from 1,500 to 500 API calls in 15 days, dropped the screen-recording requirement.
- DPDP Act creates a compliance-first opening — India's new data law (18-month phased window) rewards a tool built India-compliant from day one.

## 12. Business Model
Simple, usage-tiered SaaS — pricing set with design partners, not guessed up front.
- **Starter** — up to ₹10L/mo revenue: core dashboard, GA4 + 1 ad platform, WhatsApp cart-recovery view
- **Growth** — ₹10L–₹1Cr/mo revenue: everything in Starter + payments/logistics data + AI plain-language insights
- **Scale** — ₹1Cr+/mo revenue: everything in Growth + multi-brand/multi-store + priority support

Triple Whale and Putler's published tiers give a rough comparable range; our numbers aren't locked — the founder conversations already underway are meant to pressure-test willingness-to-pay first.
